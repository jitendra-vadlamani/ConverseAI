# Acme Robotics employee handbook (excerpt)

## Leave
Full-time employees get 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to
5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; the rest expire on
31 March. Parental leave is 26 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and
6 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams must overlap between
11:00 and 15:00 IST. Fully remote arrangements need approval from a director.

## Equipment
Each employee receives a laptop refresh every 36 months. The home-office
stipend is INR 25,000, paid once, claimable within the first 90 days.

## Expenses
Expenses above INR 10,000 need pre-approval from the employee's manager.
Claims must be filed within 30 days with itemised receipts. Travel is booked
in economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours.
