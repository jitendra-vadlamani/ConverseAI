Postmortem: checkout outage on 14 August 2026

Summary: Between 09:12 and 10:47 UTC (95 minutes) about 38% of checkout
requests failed with HTTP 503.

Root cause: a configuration change reduced the payment service's database
connection pool from 200 to 20 connections. Under morning traffic the pool was
exhausted and requests timed out.

Detection: the on-call engineer was paged by the error-rate alert at 09:19,
seven minutes after impact began.

Resolution: the change was rolled back at 10:41; error rates returned to
normal by 10:47.

Action items:
1. Add a pool-size lower bound check to the config validator (owner: Priya).
2. Alert on connection-pool saturation above 80% (owner: Marcus).
3. Run a load test before shipping any payment-service config change (owner: Wei).
